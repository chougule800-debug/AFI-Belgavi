About AFI Belagavi

Know more about the AYUSH Federation of India

AFI (Ayush Federation of India) is a Doctors Association bringing together Ayurveda, Yoga & Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha and Homeopathy professionals to promote excellence in healthcare, professional growth and public welfare. The Belagavi Taluka chapter is committed to organizing continuing education, health awareness camps, and community outreach programs that strengthen the practice of traditional medicine while embracing modern research and integrative approaches.

Our Mission

To empower AYUSH practitioners of Belagavi Taluka by fostering unity, offering continuing medical education, and promoting integrative healthcare that serves every section of the community with compassion and excellence.

Our Vision

To be the leading, most trusted federation of AYUSH doctors in Belagavi — advancing traditional medicine through innovation, education, ethics, and unwavering service to society.
